Motif.AI
========

Double-click the item for your computer:

  macOS     Motif Setup.app
  Windows   Motif Setup.pyw   (or Motif Setup.bat)
  Linux     Motif Setup.command

Setup adds Motif to MuseScore and sets it to start with your
computer. Then open MuseScore and choose Motif.AI from the
Plugins menu.

To remove Motif, open Setup again and choose Remove.
