Motif.AI
========

Double-click the item for your computer:

  macOS     Motif Setup.app
  Windows   Motif Setup.bat   (or Motif Setup.pyw)
  Linux     Motif Setup.command

Setup adds Motif to MuseScore and sets it to start with your
computer. Then open MuseScore and choose Motif.AI from the
Plugins menu (in MuseScore 4 it is under Composing/arranging
tools; if it is missing there, switch it on under Home > Plugins).

Motif's composer runs entirely on your computer: no account, no
internet connection, and nothing you write or open is ever sent
anywhere.

To remove Motif, open Setup again and choose Remove. The scores it
wrote stay in the .motif folder in your home folder.
